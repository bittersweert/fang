Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Your Privacy

Your privacy is respected. Fang is designed to provide logging, verification-record, moderation, and server-management functionality while respecting the privacy and safety of Discord users.

Information Fang May Process

To provide its features, Fang may process limited Discord-related information necessary for logging and moderation, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, staff or moderator IDs, moderation or verification actions, verification methods such as ID or Cross Verification, and relevant timestamps.

ID Verification

Fang may record that an authorized staff member completed an ID verification. Fang is not intended to permanently store identification documents, photographs of IDs, ID numbers, legal names, home addresses, or other unnecessary sensitive information contained on an identification document.

Safety & Misuse

Information handled through Fang must not be used to harass, threaten, expose, exploit, or endanger another member. We do not tolerate the use of Fang for activities that violate Discord's Terms of Service or Community Guidelines. Abuse of Fang or its data may result in restriction or a permanent ban from using the bot.

Purpose

Information processed by Fang is used to provide its logging, verification-record, moderation, security, and server-management features. We aim to collect and retain only information reasonably necessary for these functions.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Fang's features develop. Continued use of Fang after an update constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
